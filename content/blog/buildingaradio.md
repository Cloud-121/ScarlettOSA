## The Grumpy Board, How it started, and the many mistakes I made.

On June 25th, I first spoke with Conrad also known as Grumpydad; After helping build the Gulf Coast Mesh it had come to both us that the perfect Meshcore repeater didn't excist. Conrad spent over a month collecting people's thoughts on what people wanted in the "Perfect Meshcore Repeater".

What everyone agreed on was that the perfect repeater needed to have 1w output power, the ability to handle it's battery's going completly low due too little sun with being able to recover, and with a reliable way to monitor it's batteries.
